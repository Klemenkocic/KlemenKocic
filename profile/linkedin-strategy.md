# LinkedIn Content Strategy — Klemen Kocic

## Positioning line

I build AI systems that keep humans in the loop — and I write about what I learn.

## Voice rules

- Short sentences. Em dashes. Triads.
- Open with a concrete claim or scene, never "In today's world..."
- End with awareness, not a sales pitch
- **No:** "passionate," "innovative," "thrilled," "game-changer," exclamation marks
- **Yes:** "shipped," "built," "clarity," "momentum," "simple," "honest," "I learned..."
- Hook → Context → Insight → Takeaway. No CTAs unless natural.
- Every post should pass: "Would Klemen actually say this out loud?"

## Post format

**Length:** 150–250 words. LinkedIn penalises posts over 300 words in the feed.

**Structure:**
1. **Hook** (1 sentence) — concrete, specific, slightly surprising
2. **Context** (2–3 sentences) — what happened, what I was building, what I noticed
3. **Insight** (2–3 sentences) — the framework, the lesson, the reframe
4. **Takeaway** (1 sentence) — end with awareness. A thought to sit with, not a link to click.

**Formatting:**
- Line breaks between sections for readability on mobile
- No hashtags in the body. 3–5 relevant hashtags at the very end, separated by a line break.
- No emojis in the text. Clean, adult, professional.

## Engagement approach

- Comment on 3–5 posts per week from people in AI consulting, product building, and team leadership
- Comments should add substance — a question, a reframe, or a concrete example. Never "Great post!"
- Reply to every comment on own posts within 24 hours
- Share others' work when it's genuinely good, with 1–2 sentences on why

## Metrics (check monthly)

- Post impressions (baseline: first 3 posts)
- Profile views (should trend upward)
- Connection requests from target audience (consultants, founders, product people)
- Inbound messages referencing content
