## Core thesis

Augmentation produces returns that automation cannot.

When execution becomes abundant — and it will — the scarce resource migrates upward. Not to "strategy" in the abstract, but to human intent: the ability to decide what deserves to exist, why it matters, and what trade-offs you're willing to make.

AI raises the floor. It does not raise the ceiling. The ceiling is still human judgement, taste, and the willingness to commit to a direction before the data tells you to.
