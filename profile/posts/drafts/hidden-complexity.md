# Post — Hidden Complexity

**Status:** Draft
**Pillar:** Augmentation Thinking

---

I have a lot of respect for what Cal.ai did.

You take a photo of your food. It tells you the calories. People don't need to know how many calories are in a gram of carbohydrate. They don't need to understand metabolic adaptation. They need to lose weight. Cal.ai delivered that, and it worked for a lot of people. I respect that.

I think about this a lot building Charles.

Strength training is deeply scientific. Periodisation, volume landmarks, deload protocols, movement balance, recovery markers. I spent years learning all of it. Most of the coaching industry gatekeeps this knowledge, even if unintentionally. It hides behind certifications and language and price.

But the person training at 6am before work needs a programme that works, adapts when life gets in the way, and tells them what to do next. They do not need to study undulating periodisation to get stronger.

That is what AI makes possible. Hiding the complexity behind something simple that actually works. The science lives in the system. The user just doesn't have to carry it anymore.

I think a lot of expertise is moving in this direction. Things that used to be sold as consulting are becoming infrastructure. The advantage is shifting from knowing things to building the systems that hold the knowing.
