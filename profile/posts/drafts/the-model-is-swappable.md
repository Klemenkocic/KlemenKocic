# Post — The Model is Swappable

**Status:** Draft
**Pillar:** Augmentation Thinking

---

I think users should be able to choose which LLM they trust and what data they give to it.

Right now most AI products make that decision for you. They pick a model, send your data to it, and you have no say in which provider sees your information or how that decision was made.

In the systems I build, the model is a swappable component. The structure around it, the data, the filters, the validation, the feedback loop, that is the product. The model is interchangeable. And I think that is how it should be.

There is a bigger idea here too. I think in the future, AI systems will need to provide a trail of how they made a decision. Which model was used, what data was sent, what came back. So that the data is used safely, given responsibly, and with consent.

Tying your product to one provider is the new vendor lock-in. Models change. Pricing changes. Capabilities shift. But more importantly, users deserve to know where their information goes. Building the orchestration layer so the model can be swapped is a technical decision. It is also an ethical one.
