# Post — The Planetarium

**Status:** Draft
**Pillar:** Building in Public

---

I've been thinking about what working with AI should actually feel like.

Right now it's text boxes. Chat windows. Tabs. You type something, you get something back, you copy it somewhere else. It works but it doesn't feel like the future.

Here's what I think the future looks like.

You're sitting in a dark room. Around you, at different depths, coloured presences float. Each one is an agent. Each one is alive. There is no text on the screen, no chat window, no app.

When one is working, it breathes — a slow pulse of muted teal. When one needs you, it glows warm white and drifts closer. Documents appear when needed, suspended in the space between you and the agent that surfaced them, and disappear when they are no longer relevant.

You don't switch between applications. You don't type into a box. You sit at the centre of a field of intelligence, and the field arranges itself around what matters to you right now.

We call this The Planetarium. It is the design direction we're working toward. Every piece we're building now, the coordination, the memory, the governance, is infrastructure for this.

The interface we have today is a scaffold. The experience we're building toward is a room.
