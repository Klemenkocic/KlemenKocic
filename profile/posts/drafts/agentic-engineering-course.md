I just finished the Agentic Engineering course from Industrial Logic.

If you want to actually understand things like:

- Context and memory stores that give an agent real knowledge of your codebase
- Deterministic hooks and guardrails that catch the agent before it touches something it shouldn't
- Mutation testing that proves your tests would catch a real bug instead of just passing
- Dead code and duplication detection
- Architecture tests that enforce your boundaries

I'd really suggest you reach out to them.

Especially if you're an organization trying to give your developers a better understanding of AI safety, and the deterministic things you can do today to ship fast and safe.
