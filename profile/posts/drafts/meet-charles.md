# Post — Meet Charles

**Status:** Draft
**Pillar:** Building in Public

---

For the past few weeks I've been sharing pieces of what I'm building. The coaching background. The data structure problem. Surgical AI. Structural enforcement. The whole picture comes together in an app called Charles.

Charles is an AI strength training coach. You tell it about yourself, your experience, your equipment, your injuries, your goals. It builds a personalised, periodised training programme. Not a workout. A programme with phases that build on each other.

After every session, you log what you did. Charles reads that feedback and adjusts. Shoulder bothering you, it swaps the overhead press for something safer. Vacation with resistance bands, it rebuilds the week. Came back stronger than expected, it pushes you faster.

It learns. It adapts. It gets better the more you use it.

I built this because I used to do it for people, in a gym, by hand. And I knew most of what I did as a coach could be systematised if the data was structured the right way and the AI was used in the right places.

Charles is on the App Store now. It is not perfect. It is the worst version it will ever be. If you want to try it, reach out.
