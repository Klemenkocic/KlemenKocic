# Post — The Coordination Problem

**Status:** Ready to post
**Pillar:** Building in Public

---

The hard part of working with AI is the coordination.

How do you hand things back and forth between yourself and the machine? When do you step in, when do you let it run, when do you stop it?

I haven't really seen anyone talk about this. Most of the advice is about prompting. But prompting is the easy part. The design problem is the handoff. The back and forth. Knowing what stays with you and what you give to the machine.

I've been building multi-agent systems where this is basically the whole job. And it turns out it's the same problem as leading a team. Clear handoffs, explicit communication, and never assuming the other side knows what you're thinking.

I don't know if that's a new idea. But it's what I spend most of my time on.
