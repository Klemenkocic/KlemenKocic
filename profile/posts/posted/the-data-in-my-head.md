# Post — The Data in My Head

**Status:** Posted
**Pillar:** Building in Public

---

I've always been interested in training. Even while I was working in tech it was always there. At some point I decided to go do it and I became a personal trainer and nutrition coach.

When I was coaching, everything was based on knowledge in my head. How to programme the next years of training for someone to get as strong as they can. How to adjust when injuries show up. How to shift things when someone's goals change or life gets in the way.

Now I'm building an app called Charles, an AI strength training coach. And the first technical problem I had to solve had nothing to do with AI. It was about the information in my head.

I had to take what lived in there and segment it into something a system could reason over. Exercise selections, periodisation logic, movement patterns, progression models, nutrition profiles.

Each piece needed its own structure. Some of it became databases. Some of it became prompts. Some of it actually stayed with me.

And that is where most of my time is actually being spent when creating Charles. On making what I know precise enough that a system can actually use it. Every detail has to be real and defined. I'm still finding things in my head that I haven't structured yet.
