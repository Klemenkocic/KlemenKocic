# Post — The Racing Yacht

**Status:** Posted
**Pillar:** Augmentation Thinking

---

Steve Jobs called the computer a bicycle for the mind. You pedal harder, you go faster. Individual effort, multiplied.

I've been thinking about what that metaphor looks like in the age of AI.

AI is more like wind than a bicycle. It's everywhere, it's powerful, and pedalling harder doesn't help you use it. You need sails. You need a crew. You need to learn to read something you can't see.

That's the shift I tried to capture in The Racing Yacht. A racing yacht moves because the crew is in sync. Reading the conditions together, adjusting constantly. The skill is coordination.

I see the same thing in the multi-agent systems I work on. No single agent is brilliant. What works is the coordination between them. And that part has to be designed, maintained, and thought through. Same as every good team I've ever been on.

I think AI multiplies relation more than effort. And I think most people haven't really figured that out yet.
