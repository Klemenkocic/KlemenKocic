# Post — What Are You Scaling

**Status:** Posted
**Pillar:** Augmentation Thinking
**Type:** Blog post promotion

---

I co-wrote a new piece with Barton Friedland and Mark Béliczky.

The thing I find so interesting is what happens between the input and the output. The AI part in the middle is fast, consistent, scalable. But it can't tell you if the whole thing is pointed at the right thing. That part is still human.

And before you commit to a direction, you need as much real information as possible. Because once the system is running, it's not going to stop and ask if the goal still makes sense. It's just going to keep optimising.
