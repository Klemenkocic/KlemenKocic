I co-wrote a new piece with Barton Friedland and Mark Béliczky.

The thing I find so interesting is what happens between the AI and the human. The AI gives you output. Fast, consistent, scalable. But it can't tell you if the output is pointed at the right thing. That's judgement.

And before you commit to a direction, you need as much real information as possible. Because once the system is running, it's not going to stop and ask if the goal still makes sense. It's just going to keep optimising.
