## Section 1: Headline

**CURRENT:**
> Consultant @ Luminous Group | Designing AI-augmented decision systems for measurable performance | Co-Founder @ ViaVia.Travel

**PROBLEM:**
The middle clause is dense jargon. "Designing AI-augmented decision systems for measurable performance" takes 3 reads to parse. A recruiter, investor, or potential client scanning LinkedIn gives you maybe 8 words before deciding to keep reading. Right now those 8 words are "Consultant @ Luminous Group | Designing AI-augmented..." and they've already moved on.

**REWRITE OPTIONS (pick one):**

**Option A — Clean & direct:**
> Consultant @ Luminous Group | Co-Founder @ ViaVia.Travel | Helping teams use AI to make better decisions, faster

**Option B — Emphasis on the "builds" part:**
> Consultant @ Luminous Group | Co-Founder @ ViaVia.Travel | I design how AI fits into real teams — then build it

**Option C — Shortest, highest clarity:**
> AI Consultant (Luminous Group) · Co-Founder (ViaVia.Travel)

**My recommendation: Option A.** It's clear, scannable, and the last clause tells someone exactly what you do without needing a glossary. "Helping teams use AI to make better decisions, faster" — that's a sentence a CEO understands, an investor understands, and a recruiter can pattern-match against.
