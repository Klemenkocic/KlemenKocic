# Post — The Racing Yacht

**Status:** Draft
**Pillar:** Augmentation Thinking

---

Steve Jobs called the computer a bicycle for the mind. You pedal harder, you go faster. Individual effort, multiplied.

I think that metaphor is broken now.

AI isn't a bicycle. It's more like wind. It's everywhere, it's powerful, and pedalling harder doesn't help you use it. You need sails. You need a crew. You need to learn to read something you can't see.

That's what I wrote about in The Racing Yacht. The shift from individual effort to collective coordination. A racing yacht doesn't move because one person works harder. It moves because the crew is in sync, reading the conditions together, adjusting constantly.

I see this in my own work. The multi-agent system I built at Luminous doesn't work because any single agent is brilliant. It works because the coordination between them is clear. Same as every good team I've ever been on.

AI multiplies relation, not just effort. And I think most organisations haven't figured that out yet.
