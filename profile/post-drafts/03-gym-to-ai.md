# Post 3 — Gym to AI

**Pillar:** The Non-Linear Path
**Scheduled:** April W1
**Status:** Draft

---

Six months ago I was coaching a CEO through a deadlift in a Berlin studio. Now I build agent infrastructure for an AI consultancy in Munich.

People ask how those connect. Here's the honest answer: they connect through reading people.

In a gym, you learn to watch before you speak. Is this person distracted? Are they holding back? Do they need a push — or do they need you to shut up and let them figure it out?

That skill doesn't stay in the gym. It shows up in every consulting engagement, every standup, every client workshop.

The path from personal training to AI consulting wasn't a pivot. It was a stack. Seoul taught me async communication and timezone discipline. Gaming taught me how to ship under pressure at real scale. Coaching taught me patience — and that the best intervention is usually a good question, not a good answer.

None of it was planned. All of it was useful.

I've noticed that the most interesting people I work with have non-linear backgrounds too. The designer who used to be a chef. The engineer who studied philosophy. The founder who coached athletes.

The unusual combination is the differentiator. Not despite the detours — because of them.
