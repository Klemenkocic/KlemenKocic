# Post — The Gates

**Status:** Posted
**Pillar:** Building in Public

---

We built a system at ViaVia with seven AI departments. Product, R&D, Finance, Business, Design, Marketing, Engineering. Each one has agents that do real work. Market research, financial projections, competitor analysis, logo generation, Flutter code.

The part we spent the most time on was the gates.

Every workflow has a point where everything stops and waits for a human. The AI does the analysis, produces the deliverable, and then it goes to "Awaiting Decision" in Linear. Nothing moves until someone comments. GO, NO-GO, or PIVOT.

The decisions are the actual product. Whether to build this thing. Whether the design is right. Whether it's ready to ship. Those are judgement calls, not optimisation problems.

The first app we put through it took a while. The second one went from idea to a full product spec and design in a couple of sessions. Same framework, same gates, same humans deciding. Just faster.

I think the interesting design problem with AI is how you keep humans in the right places while letting it handle everything else. For now, Linear comments as approval gates is a really good solution. I'm certain that will change soon.
