# Post — What Are You Scaling (Repost)

**Status:** Posted
**Pillar:** Augmentation Thinking
**Type:** Repost of Mark Béliczky's "What You Govern Determines What You Become"

---

I really liked working on this piece with Mark and Barton.

The thing I find so interesting is what happens between the AI and the human. The AI gives you output. Fast, consistent, scalable. But it can't tell you if the output is pointed at the right thing. That's judgement. And judgement is the part that doesn't scale.

I think about this a lot in my own work honestly. The more I automate, the more the important part becomes knowing what to automate and what to leave alone.
