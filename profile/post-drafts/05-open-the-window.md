# Post — Open the Window

**Status:** Draft
**Pillar:** Augmentation Thinking

---

I wrote this piece because I kept hearing the same thing. "Oh that was written by AI." "That looks like AI." People pointing it out like it's a gotcha.

And I think we're past that now. Of course it's written with AI. Of course many things will be in the future. That's not the interesting question anymore.

The interesting question is: what do you keep for yourself?

Not everything should go through a model. Some things are yours. The direction, the taste, the judgement call that only you can make because you've been in the room, you know the people, you've done the work.

These posts I write on LinkedIn, for example. These are mine. I'm writing them. Not because AI couldn't draft something, but because the whole point is that it's my thinking, not a polished version of everyone's thinking.

The shift isn't from "don't use AI" to "use AI." It's from "use AI for everything" to "decide what stays human."
